## About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the **University of Tsukuba** and a member of the **Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (C-AIR)**. I am also a Visiting Scientist with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and the **Imageomics Institute at The Ohio State University**.

My research develops machine-learning methods that are interpretable, efficient, and useful for scientific discovery. I am particularly interested in the intersection of **machine learning and biology**.
