I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the Institute of Systems and Information Engineering, [University of Tsukuba](https://www.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/), and a member of the [Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (C-AIR)](https://air.tsukuba.ac.jp/en/). I am also a Visiting Scientist with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and the Imageomics Institute at [The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu/).

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Imageomics Institute, The Ohio State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Patna, where my thesis focused on advanced machine-learning algorithms for streaming data.

My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning and biology**, with interests in computer vision, imageomics, explainable AI, AI for health, streaming data, real-time summarization, and foundation models.

## Research interests

- Machine learning and computer vision
- Imageomics and AI for science
- Explainable and interpretable AI
- AI in health
- Streaming data and real-time summarization
- Foundation models
